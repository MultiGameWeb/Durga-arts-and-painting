A little about us

Thoughtful art. Made with care.

Durga Arts and Paintings has been creating wall murals and canvas paintings in Hyderabad for over 15 years. The work includes school and play school murals, custom interior wall art and canvas paintings.

From colourful learning themes to decorative artwork, each project starts with understanding the space and the idea you want to bring to it.

Before you get in touch.

Have a different question? Send a message and describe your project. You can also share a photo of the wall you have in mind.

Ask on WhatsApp ↗
What types of painting work do you do?

Services include school wall murals, play school paintings, interior wall art and canvas paintings.

Do you take custom painting requests?

Yes. Get in touch and describe the subject, style or theme you have in mind so the requirements can be discussed.

How can I ask for a quotation?

Send a WhatsApp message with a short description and, where possible, a photo of the wall or space. You can then discuss the idea and pricing.

Which location do you serve?

Durga Arts and Paintings is based in Hyderabad. Contact the team to discuss your project location.
